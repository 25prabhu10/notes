## Lenses

Types of lenses:

- Prime lens: A lens with a fixed focal length (e.g., 50mm, 85mm). Known for sharpness and wide apertures
- Zoom lens: A lens with a variable focal length (e.g., 24-70mm). Offers versatility but may have a smaller maximum aperture
- Wide-angle lens: A lens with a short focal length (e.g., 16mm, 24mm). Ideal for landscapes and architecture
- Telephoto lens: A lens with a long focal length (e.g., 70-200mm). Great for wildlife and sports photography
- Macro lens: A lens designed for close-up photography, allowing you to capture fine details of small subjects
